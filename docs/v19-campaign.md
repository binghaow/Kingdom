# v19 campaign usability, training and aviation

## Behavior

* Selected units and buildings expose effective age, attack, armor, range, speed and attack interval. A details dialog shows HP, population, cost, training time, role, counter multipliers and a building's complete roster. Production cards include attack/armor and an explicit information affordance.
* Arsenal → range/barracks/stable/town production; Congress → range; Shipyard → unified dock. The retired structures are excluded from new construction and existing local saves/live network states migrate. Existing paid queues persist. National academies retain exclusive roles and doctrines. Engineers explicitly build faster and repair rather than gather.
* Ten distinct national hero abilities have real bounded damage/healing/buffs, mana/gold costs and cooldowns. The KC book permanently unlocks four independently cooled spells for current and future heroes. Reward claims focus an existing hero; the command strip clearly identifies an equipped book.
* Boarding orders direct ground passengers toward a transport. Capacities, hostile ownership, landing terrain and safety checks remain authoritative. Mobile transport/hero/carrier selections keep their command panel open. Unloading cancels pending pickup requests.
* Final-age bomber planes and heavy airships fly over targets to drop delayed area bombs. Planes are faster and carry two bombs; gunships carry six. Empty aircraft return to an airship works or parent carrier and spend five seconds rearming before resuming orders. Aircraft carriers support two separately paid carrier planes. Carrier attack orders launch their aircraft; melee cannot target aircraft. Aeronautics and Carrier Aviation require Age V and actual research payments.
* Movement uses quiet recorded footsteps, real horse hooves, wind/cloth or water instead of repeating spoken move acknowledgements. Worker/soldier selection uses male replies; selection responses are throttled. Voices no longer play for every generic command or completed unit queue.
* Seventeen isolated tutorials cover goals, selection, movement, gathering, construction, production roles, counters, research, transport, heroes, spellbook, siege, aviation and victory. Each has an objective-driven green completion check stored separately. Practice never overwrites or clears the saved campaign; lesson victory exits back to the catalog.
* Actual income, training, kills, losses, damage, peak population and bounded time-series records persist. Results compare every nation and show income curves. Opponent statistics are excluded from online snapshots until battle ends. Old saves begin recording after this update.
* Settings use a consistent grid, with restart separated visually. Chinese/English support includes the new training, abilities and statistics.

## Validation

Full pre-existing regression suite; 11 campaign cases covering merged production, saves, embark capacity, national magic, rewards, overhead bombing, rearm, carrier production and authoritative ownership; all 17 tutorial scenarios completed through real simulation actions under one minute with original-state restoration. Browser checks: actual spell cast and green check, real ship boarding/unloading, English catalog, actual victory and results dialog, responsive layouts. Device speaker output on a physical iPhone remains outside this desktop-browser verification.

Built-in ImageGen mode with transparent_background=true. Generated PNGs are copied unchanged into the project. Runtime Canvas cuts atlas cells and animates effects; no offline image-editing scripts were used. Code-native biplane meshes, hinged gates and live particles remain procedural.
