# v22: coherent terrain, combat alerts and sealed walls

Actual hostile damage now updates at most four own-side minimap markers, which pulse while damage continues. One short recorded bugle cue plays per attack episode, resetting after eight quiet seconds. Mere enemy proximity remains a text warning, avoiding duplicate attack sounds. The horn and steel impacts accompany directional attack chevrons and brighter local sparks. Multiplayer snapshots send only the recipient's damage markers.

Fresh worlds use geography revision 2: rivers scale with map dimensions, central lakes and oxbow lakes use substantial relative dimensions, coast maps retain extensive open water and a separated KC island. Connected-water cleanup removes tiny isolated water components and narrow remnants. Bridge decks allow land units across while the water beneath remains navigable. Cliffs form overlapping ridges and avoid lake interiors. Actual harvestable woods form home stands and long forest belts; bounded extra trees and grouped tree sprites provide density without unbounded generation.

Single-player and multiplayer creation offer Standard or Rich mineral reserves. Rich doubles land gold and stone deposit amounts and maximum amounts, leaving starting banks, gathering rates and marine resources unchanged. The setting persists in saves, room configuration and private snapshots.

Wall collision and rendering share explicit primary/stub rectangles. Cardinal neighbors create joined corners even when the corner node's original orientation differs. Picking masks include the junction signature. Movement sweeps each step against walls, so cached paths and long steps cannot tunnel through new construction. Friendly gate passage, enemy exclusion and destroyed openings remain intact.

Existing campaigns retain their prior geographical layout, buildings and resources to avoid flooding or displacing player settlements. Start a new campaign to apply the new world generation and reserve setting. Wall collision and battle feedback updates also apply when loading older saves.

Validation: all existing regression groups passed. Fog-memory and air-water test fixtures now isolate vision and locate water from terrain rather than assuming a fixed coordinate remains water after the redesign. New checks cover doubled ore without increased banks, persistence and room configuration, real forest belts, all nine maps without tiny sampled waterbodies, bridge land/water traversal, a completely sealed wall ring, stale-path sweep collision, joined corners, allied/enemy gates and destroyed openings, attack-episode deduplication and multiplayer marker privacy. Browser QA covers rich-reserve setup, actual broad-lake/forest rendering, real tutorial combat alert, wall junction rendering and recorded short-alarm loading. No physical mobile-device performance claim is made.
