# v21: Guardian performance and three tactical maps

KC dragon and demon now have dedicated idle, charging/flame and melee sprites, animation state tied to actual attacks, target facing and a moving mouth/hand fire origin. Existing telegraph time, damage, age restriction, bounded area targets and remote single guardian remain intact. Selection, charge, flame, melee and heavy footsteps use distinct layered physical recordings. Provenance and CC0 links are in public/audio/CREDITS.md; no film soundtrack was extracted.

Nine maps total: Tidal Archipelago emphasizes island resources, pearl shoals, escort fleets and landings; Caldera Rift adds broken ring cliffs and passes for infantry/artillery with aerial shortcuts; Three-Fork Lowlands mixes river crossings, lakes and cavalry flanks. These three layouts scale for 2–5 local nations and are valid in 2–4 person multiplayer. Initial home resources and marine pearl budgets are equal. Fishing boats gather food at fisheries and gold at pearl shoals; villagers cannot gather marine deposits. Combat ships escort rather than gather. Aircraft retain final-age research and ranged anti-air weaknesses.

Checks: all existing regression groups passed after adding new-map localization to the dictionary contract. Dedicated tests cover new maps at 2/3/4/5 nations, equal home budgets, water-resource validity, exactly one remote guardian, actual pearl income, air/cliff restrictions, save restoration, guardian pose/facing transitions and eight recorded cues. Browser checks cover pose changes and alpha, sound control loading, bilingual map selection and actual map/KC rendering. No physical iPhone/iPad verification in this update.

First generation had an unsuitable translucent background; the built-in edit removed it, with zero-alpha corner and inter-cell pixels verified. Atlas copied unchanged; runtime crops individual cells and animates transforms.
